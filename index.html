Hackathon Presentation Script

Problem: AI-Driven Market Linkage & Smart Cataloging for Marginalized Artisans

30-Second Pitch for Judges

"Respected judges! We built Artisans360 to solve two major problems faced by marginalized rural artisans across Gujarat (Silk Bandhani, Pottery, Kutch Handloom, Sankheda Woodcraft & Copper Bells): 1) The Digital Literacy Barrier: Using our AI Smart Cataloging Studio, non-literate artisans simply snap a photo, and the AI automatically computes fair labor pricing, generates heritage stories, and speaks it out via audio. 2) The Exploitative Middleman Barrier: We provide direct market linkage within a 25 km circle around Bardoli with individual user logins, Multi-item shopping cart & 4-step secure checkout, artisan workshop open/close control, and direct verified local delivery partner fulfillment."

Key Features to Demo Live:
1. AI Smart Cataloging & Photo Auto-Fill

Artisans snap or upload craft photos and AI automatically generates fair prices, stories, and heritage details.

2. Shopping Cart & Step-by-Step Checkout

Add crafts to cart, review address & order summary, choose COD/UPI, and get live OTP delivery tracking.
